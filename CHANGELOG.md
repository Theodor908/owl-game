# Changelog

Every published build, newest first. The version is for people; the build number is what the game's updates go by.

## 0.3.0 alpha, build 16 (2026-11-23)

Shorter matches: the category shows with the question, a quicker answer reveal, and battle damage grows from turn 16

## 0.2.0 alpha, build 15 (2026-11-23)

Time to read each question before the answers open, and reflect is capped at 60 percent except for Herophilus at 80, with weaker mirror cards

## 0.1.1 alpha, build 14 (2026-11-23)

The account screen says Connecting to the server instead of the address

## 0.1.1 alpha, build 13 (2026-11-23)

The lobby code can be copied, and the version shows in the corner

## 0.1.0 alpha, builds 1 to 12 (2026-11-18 to 2026-11-23)

The first playable game, before versions were named.

- The rules: shop, tavern tiers, board and hand, golden cards and their rewards on placement, twelve heroes, battles of six questions, the endgame, reflect, armor, healing, eight difficulty cards.
- Offline play against five bots, a guided first match, and online lobbies with friends, joined by code.
- About a thousand questions in ten categories, each from a quiz with an answer key and checked against a second reference.
- The shop laid out like an auto-battler, with the hand as a fan; damage and reflect effects; music and sound.
- A server with accounts, guests, friends, a download page, limits against abuse, and a privacy notice players confirm.
- The game updates itself from the server, with every download signed.
