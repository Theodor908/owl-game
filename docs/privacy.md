# Owl privacy notice

Version 3.

Last changed: 23 November 2026.

Owl is a private, non-commercial game run by one person for friends. This notice says what the game server keeps about you and what you can do about it.

- Who runs it: the friend who gave you this game. It is not offered to the public, so if you have it, you know them.
- Contact: reach them the way you usually do. There is no separate address for the game.

## What is kept

| What | Why | How it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Your username | So friends can find and recognise you | Encrypted, plus a keyed fingerprint used to look it up |
| Your password | To log you in | Only as a one-way hash; nobody can read it back, including the person who runs the server |
| Your friends and friend requests | To let you invite each other | As links between accounts |
| When your account was made, and when and which version of this notice you confirmed | To know which version of this notice you saw | As dates and a version number |
| When you last used your account | To erase accounts nobody uses any more | As a date |
| Your open sessions | To keep you logged in | As a fingerprint of a random token, with the dates it was opened and last used |
| Your internet address, for a short time | To stop one device from guessing passwords or flooding the server | In the server's memory only, with a count of recent tries, for at most an hour. Never written to disk or to a log, and not linked to your account |

Nothing else is kept. There is no e-mail address, no real name, no location, no device details, no advertising and no analytics. The server never stores or logs your internet address: it holds it in memory only while it counts towards a limit, and forgets it after at most an hour, or when the server restarts. Matches are not stored: when a match ends, it is gone.

## Playing as a guest

You can play without an account. The server then makes up a name for you (such as `Guest_7K2QX`) and keeps only that name, your friend links and your session. There is no password. All of it is erased when you choose "Stop being a guest", or when your session runs out.

## Why this is allowed

Keeping this data is necessary to give you the account you asked for and to let you play with friends (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), performance of a contract). Without a username and a password there is no account.

Holding your internet address for a short time keeps the server safe for everyone who plays on it (Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interest). It is used for nothing else.

## Who else sees it

Other players see your username, and whether you are online if they are your friends. The data is not sold or shared. The server is reached through Cloudflare (Cloudflare Tunnel), which carries the traffic between your device and the server and is able to see it.

## How long it is kept

Until you delete your account, or until a year has passed without you using it: an account nobody has logged in with or played on for a year is erased, with its friend links and sessions. A session ends 30 days after you logged in, or after 14 days without use. No backups of the accounts are made, so a deleted account is gone at once and for good. It also means that if the server's storage fails, every account is lost and has to be made again.

## What you can do

All of these are in the game, under Profile:

- **See and take your data:** "Export my data" gives you everything listed above as a file. Your internet address is not in it: it is not linked to your account, and it is gone within the hour by itself.
- **Correct it:** you can change your username and your password.
- **Erase it:** "Delete my account" removes your account, your sessions and your friend links at once.

You can also ask the person who runs the server, who must answer within a month. You have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Romania that is ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

## When this notice changes

The game carries a copy of this notice, so you can read it without a connection. When the notice changes, its version number goes up. The next time you open the game online you are shown the new version, and you play online again once you have confirmed it.

## Age

Owl accounts are for people aged 16 or over.

## If something goes wrong

There is no e-mail address to write to you at. If the data is ever exposed, you will be told in the game the next time you log in, and your password will be reset.

## Lost password

Because there is no e-mail address, a forgotten password cannot be reset by you. Ask the person who runs the server; they can delete the account so you can make it again.
