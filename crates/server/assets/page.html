About the game

Owl is a card battler where the fighting is done with what you know.

Up to six players share a lobby. Each round you visit the shop, buy cards and place them on your board. Then you meet one opponent over six multiple-choice questions. Answer more of them right and you deal the damage. The last player standing wins.

Cards bend a battle your way: they pull questions toward the categories you know best, and add answer time, armor or damage. Three cards of one category earn a bonus.

  • Play with friends online: open a lobby and share its code.
  • Play offline against bots, any time.
  • Ten categories, from History to Sport.
  • Twelve heroes, each with an ability of their own.
  • No ads and no tracking.
